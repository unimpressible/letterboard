Getting it onto your real profile

Backgrounds, avatars, frames and mini-profiles

  1. Each item in your shopping list links to its Points Shop page. Buy it with Steam Points (you earn them by buying games on Steam).
  2. On Steam, open your profile, choose Edit Profile, then Profile Background, Avatar, Avatar Frame or Mini Profile, and pick the item.

Achievement showcase

  1. Buy the games in your shopping list and unlock the achievements it names. In the Zup! games, levels are named after the letter they award.
  2. In Edit Profile → Featured Showcase, add an Achievement Showcase and fill the 7 slots in the order shown here.

Item showcase

  1. Get each item: emoticons, stickers and backgrounds from the Points Shop or Community Market, trading cards from the Market or by playing the game.
  2. Add an Item Showcase and choose the items from your inventory in order.

Good to know

  • How many showcases you can add depends on your Steam level.
  • Emoticons work in your summary and in custom info boxes: type the code, like :steamhappy:.
  • Prices and Points Shop availability change. Check the item on Steam before you buy.
